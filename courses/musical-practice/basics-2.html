Find Middle C

Basics Piano

William Middle C is the most important reference note on the piano. Look for the pair of two black keys nearest the middle of the keyboard — Middle C is the white key just to the left of that pair.

Click Middle C. (Hint: it's the highlighted yellow key.)

There are several "C" notes on a piano (every octave repeats). Middle C sits in the middle of a standard 88-key piano — just left of the pair of two black keys closest to the center.
