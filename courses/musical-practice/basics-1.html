Meet the Piano

Basics Piano

William Hi! I'm William. This is a piano. It has white keys and black keys. Let's meet it together — follow each step below!
Step 1 of 4
Tap any white key to hear it.

White keys are the natural notes C D E F G A B. Black keys are sharps (♯) and flats (♭).
