Language

Èdè Yorùbá

A major Niger-Congo language spoken by 45+ million across southwestern Nigeria, Benin, Togo & the diaspora (Brazil, Cuba, Caribbean — Lucumí/Santería). Three tones (high, mid, low), nasalized vowels, rich oral literature, and Ifá divination tradition. Not taught by Duolingo. 30 units, 450 lessons.
