Scots

Lallans — the Lowland Scots language. From everyday "Whit's the craic?" to Burns's poetry. Phonology (rolled r, /ʍ/, vowel system), pronouns (ye/youse), modal verbs, irregular verbs (gae, gaed, gaen), distinctive vocabulary (bairn, dreich, glaikit, blether), regional dialects (Doric, Glaswegian, Borders, Shetland, Orcadian, Ulster Scots), spelling traditions, the Scots Wikipedia debate, Scots in literature (Burns, Stevenson, Lochhead, Welsh), Scots in song & comedy, Scots in education & signage, the Scots Language Centre, official recognition under the European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages, and modern revival efforts. 79 units, 1182 lessons.
