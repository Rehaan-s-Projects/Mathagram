📘 Read: Basketball Arc

A basketball player shoots from a distance of 5 m. The ball leaves their hands at an angle of 50° above horizontal with speed \(v_0 = 8\,\text{m/s}\). Using projectile motion, the horizontal range is \(R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}\). Plug in \(\theta = 50°\) and \(g = 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2\) to model where the ball lands.

📘 Read: Soccer Free Kick

A soccer ball is kicked at 22 m/s at 32° above the ground. The maximum height reached is \(H = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g}\). Players curve the ball using rotational angles — the wall they're aiming over is 2.4 m tall.

📘 Read: Skateboard Ramp

A skater takes a quarter-pipe with a 45° lip. The horizontal speed and vertical speed at takeoff are equal because \(\sin 45° = \cos 45°\). This makes 45° the launch angle for maximum range on flat ground.
