🎡 Read: Ferris Wheel

A Ferris wheel has radius 20 m, center 22 m above the ground, and one full rotation takes 60 seconds. The rider's height is \(h(t) = 22 - 20\cos\!\left(\tfrac{2\pi}{60}\,t\right)\). At \(t = 0\), the rider is at the bottom (\(h = 2\) m).

🌊 Read: Tides

High tide is 6 m, low tide is 1 m, and the period is roughly 12 hours. Tide height: \(H(t) = 3.5 + 2.5\sin\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{6}\,t\right)\). Midline 3.5 m, amplitude 2.5 m.

🎵 Read: Sound & Music

Middle A on a piano is \(440\,\text{Hz}\). Its sound pressure can be modeled \(p(t) = A\sin(2\pi \cdot 440\, t)\). Doubling the frequency to \(880\,\text{Hz}\) gives the A one octave up.

☀️ Read: Daylight Hours

In Boston, daylight ranges from about 9 hours (December) to 15 hours (June). A model: \(D(t) = 12 + 3\sin\!\left(\tfrac{2\pi}{365}(t-80)\right)\), where \(t\) is the day of year.
