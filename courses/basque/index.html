Euskara — Basque

Europe's only surviving language isolate — no known relatives anywhere on Earth. Spoken by ~1 million in the Basque Country (Euskal Herria) across northern Spain & southwestern France. Pre-dates the Indo-European invasion of Europe. Ergative-absolutive grammar, vigesimal counting, agglutinative morphology. Not taught by Duolingo. 30 units, 450 lessons.
