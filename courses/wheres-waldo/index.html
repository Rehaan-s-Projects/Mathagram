Where's Waldo

🔍 The world's most famous striped-shirt traveler. Find Waldo (Wally in the UK) — and his friends Wenda, Wizard Whitebeard, Odlaw, and Woof — across all four classic Martin Handford books, page by page. Beachfronts, museums, train stations, ski slopes, the Stone Age, Egyptians, Romans, Vikings, Gobbling Gluttons, Battling Monks, Underwater Wars, the Land of Waldos, Robin Hood, swordfights, science-fiction sets, and the Hollywood future. 4 units · 262 pages.
