Anatomy of the Tongue

Muscles, Frenulum & Papillae

Your tongue is a muscular hydrostat — eight muscles working without a single bone. Tiny bumps called papillae cover its surface; most are mechanical, but a few thousand carry taste buds. A thin membrane underneath, the frenulum, tethers it to the floor of your mouth.

Muscles4 intrinsic shape it, 4 extrinsic anchor & move it.
FrenulumThin membrane on the underside — keeps the tongue tethered.
PapillaeFungiform, filiform, foliate, circumvallate.
Taste Buds~2,000–8,000 of them — every region detects every taste.
