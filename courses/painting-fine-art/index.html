Painting & Fine Art

From a blank canvas to a finished painting. Color theory, the color wheel, drawing as foundation, materials & brushes, watercolor (washes, glazing, wet-on-wet), gouache, acrylic, oil painting (fat over lean, underpainting, glazing), composition (rule of thirds, golden ratio), perspective (one-, two-, three-point), light & shadow, atmospheric perspective, still life, landscape, portraiture, figure painting, plein air, the Renaissance (Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael), Baroque (Caravaggio, Rembrandt, Vermeer), Impressionism (Monet, Renoir, Degas), Post-Impressionism (Van Gogh, Cézanne, Gauguin), Cubism & Picasso, Surrealism (Dalí, Magritte), Abstract Expressionism (Pollock, Rothko), Chinese ink & wash, Japanese ukiyo-e, Indian & Persian miniatures, African & Indigenous art, encaustic, tempera & fresco, and modern digital & mixed media. 35 units, 525 lessons.
