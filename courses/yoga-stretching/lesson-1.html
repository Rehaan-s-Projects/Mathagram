Unit 1 — Yoga Foundations & History

What Is Yoga?

Yoga is a 5,000+ year-old practice that unites body, breath, and mind. The Sanskrit word yoga comes from the root yuj, meaning "to yoke" or "to join" — joining movement with breath, attention with intention.

Modern yoga has many flavours: Hatha (gentle, foundational), Vinyasa (flow), Ashtanga (athletic), Iyengar (precise alignment), Yin (long holds), Restorative (deeply relaxing) — you'll explore them all over the next few units.
