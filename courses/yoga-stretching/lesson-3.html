Unit 1 — Yoga Foundations & History

Patanjali's Yoga Sutras

Around 400 CE, the sage Patanjali compiled 196 short aphorisms — the Yoga Sutras — into four books (Padas). They became the defining text of classical yoga, codifying meditation, ethics, and the path to liberation.

The very second sutra defines yoga itself: "Yogas citta vrtti nirodhah" — "Yoga is the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind." To realize that stillness, Patanjali lays out the Eight Limbs (Ashtanga), with asana (steady seat) and dhyana (meditation) at the heart.

Today's pose is Padmasana (puhd-MAH-suh-nuh), the Lotus Pose — the classical meditation seat in which Patanjali himself is traditionally depicted. We'll modify it gently — Half Lotus is fine for beginners.
