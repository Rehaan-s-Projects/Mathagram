Unit 1 — Yoga Foundations & History

Vedic & Pre-Vedic Origins

Yoga's roots stretch back over 5,000 years. Archaeologists have found seals from the Indus Valley Civilization (Mohenjo-Daro & Harappa, c. 3300–1300 BCE) depicting figures seated in cross-legged meditation postures — the earliest visual evidence of yoga-like practice.

The first textual references come from the Vedas, a collection of Sanskrit hymns composed roughly 1500–500 BCE. The Rig Veda mentions "yoking" the senses and the breath. Centuries later, the Upanishads developed yoga into a path of inward inquiry — meditation, breath control, and self-realization.

Today's pose carries those ancient roots forward: Sukhasana (sue-KA-suh-nuh), the Easy Pose — the same cross-legged seat depicted on Indus Valley seals nearly five millennia ago.
